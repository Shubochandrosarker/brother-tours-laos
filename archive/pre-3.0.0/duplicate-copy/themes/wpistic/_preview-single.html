Overview.

North to south across Laos, by river and road, at the pace the country keeps. A journey carried by a licensed Lao host, with named hotels, named meals, and time built in to do nothing well.

Designed for the slow traveler who wants to feel Laos rather than photograph it.

Moments you'll remember.

01 A private welcome from your host on the first evening.
02 Two slow days on the river, by boat where the road would rush you.
03 A named village homestay, with a weaving lesson at the loom.
04 A cooking class in a family kitchen, not a hotel.
05 Morning light over the karst country, before the day begins.
06 A farewell meal your host plans around you.
