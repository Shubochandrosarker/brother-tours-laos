## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the website, the inquiry and booking process, and correspondence by email, WhatsApp, or other channels.

## 3. What we collect

### When you send an inquiry (Build My Trip, Contact, Travel Agents, Request Tour Availability forms)
- Your name, email address, country, and phone or WhatsApp number.
- Your travel dates, number of travelers, destination and journey interests, and any preferences or requirements you write to us.
- The messages you send us.

### When you book a journey
- The information above, plus names, dates of birth or ages of travelers where needed for the journey, passport details where required for permits, accommodation bookings, or domestic flights, and dietary or mobility needs you tell us about.
- Payment information. We do not store full card numbers on our systems; payments are handled by the payment provider we use. **[TO CONFIRM: name of payment provider(s)]**

### When you subscribe to the newsletter
- Your email address.

### When you contact us by WhatsApp, email, or phone
- Your contact details and the content of the conversation.

### When you browse the website
- Usage information through cookies and analytics: pages visited, approximate location (country/city level), device type, browser, and referral source. See our Cookie Policy for details. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. **[TO CONFIRM: any other analytics or advertising tools]**

## 4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

1. Respond to your inquiry, ask clarifying questions, and prepare itineraries and quotes.
2. Operate and manage your booking: confirm journeys, arrange accommodation, transport, guides, activities and permits with local suppliers, take payments, and handle changes or cancellations.
3. Send administrative messages about your journey (not marketing).
4. Send the newsletter, only if you subscribed, with an unsubscribe link in every message.
5. Improve our website and service.
6. Meet legal obligations, including Lao PDR tax and record-keeping requirements, and resolve disputes.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the information from inquiry or booking forms for marketing without your consent.

## 5. Legal basis for processing

We process personal information because it is necessary to:

- Take steps before a booking and perform the booking contract (inquiries, itinerary preparation, booking operation).
- Meet our legal obligations (tax records, public safety obligations).
- Support our legitimate interests, such as improving the service and responding to correspondence, where these do not override your rights.
- Comply with your consent, where we ask for it (for example, newsletter subscription, marketing photos, non-essential cookies). You can withdraw consent at any time.

## 6. Who we share data with

We share personal information only where needed to operate your journey or run the website:

- **Local suppliers** — hotels, drivers, guides, boat operators, and other service providers, only with the details they need to deliver the service.
- **Payment providers** — to process deposits and balances. **[TO CONFIRM: provider name]**
- **Website and email service providers** — hosting, email delivery, and analytics providers that process data on our behalf. **[TO CONFIRM: provider names — hosting, analytics]**
- **Authorities** — where required by law or to comply with Lao PDR regulations.

We do not share data with unrelated third parties. Where we ask a supplier to process data, the supplier receives only what is needed for your journey.

## 7. International transfers

Some suppliers and service providers operate outside Laos (for example, hotels' reservation systems, international payment providers, or hosting services). Your information may be processed in those countries. We rely on contracts and provider safeguards, and we only transfer what is needed to deliver the service you booked.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Inquiry records** (no booking): deleted or anonymized within 24 months of the last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
- **Booking records**: kept for the period required by Lao PDR tax and accounting law (draft: 5 years after the journey), to resolve questions about payments, supplier obligations, and disputes.

## 9. Cookies

The website uses essential cookies to function and, with your choice, analytics cookies. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies; you can change your choice at any time. See our Cookie Policy for details.

## 10. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
- Delete information we no longer need for the purposes in this policy.
- Stop processing for specific purposes, such as newsletter marketing.
- Withdraw consent you previously gave.

To make a request, email enquiry@brothertours.com with your name and the email or phone you used. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity. Where we delete information that suppliers need to deliver an existing booking, we will explain the impact before deleting.

## 11. Children

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without a parent or guardian's involvement. Where children travel, a parent or guardian makes the booking and provides their information. If you believe we hold information about a child without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Security

We use HTTPS encryption on the website. Personal information is accessible only to staff and suppliers who need it for the purposes above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make significant changes, we update the "Last updated" date above and, where we have your email, notify you of important changes. The version in force is the one published on this page.
