No payment is taken before you approve an itinerary. Until a booking is confirmed, prices may change (for example, if suppliers change rates or your dates change).

## 6. Changes and cancellations by us

We plan journeys carefully, but sometimes we must change or cancel a booking:

- **Minor changes** (timing adjustments, a comparable hotel change, route order) do not entitle you to a refund. We will tell you as soon as possible.
- **Significant changes** (a materially different journey) entitle you to accept the changed arrangement, choose an alternative of comparable standard where available, or cancel with a refund of amounts recoverable from suppliers.
- **Cancellation by us** before departure, other than for reasons under section 7, entitles you to a full refund of payments made to us, or an alternative journey where you prefer one.
- **After departure**, if a significant part of the services cannot be delivered, we will arrange comparable replacement services where possible, or refund the difference between the price paid and the value of services delivered.
