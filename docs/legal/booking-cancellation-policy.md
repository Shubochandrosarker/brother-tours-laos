## 1. Scope

This policy applies to journeys, day tours, guide services, and travel services booked with Brother Tours Sole Co., Ltd. (Vientiane, Lao PDR). It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. If this policy and your booking confirmation differ, the booking confirmation governs your booking.

## 2. Deposits and payment

- A booking is confirmed when you approve your itinerary and we receive the deposit stated on the itinerary (draft standard: 30% of the total). **[TO CONFIRM: standard deposit]**
- The balance is due before departure on the date in your booking confirmation (draft standard: 30 days before departure). **[TO CONFIRM: balance due date]**
- No payment is taken until you approve your itinerary.
- Payments are accepted by the methods on your invoice or payment link. Bank and provider fees are borne by the payer unless stated otherwise.

## 3. Cancellation by you — journeys (multi-day)

Cancellation notice is measured in days before the journey's departure date. Refunds are of payments made to us, less any non-recoverable third-party costs (hotels, permits, flights, or other suppliers that do not refund us).

| When you cancel | What you receive back |
|---|---|
| 30 or more days before departure | Full refund of payments made, less any non-recoverable third-party costs |
| 15–29 days before departure | 50% of payments made, less any non-recoverable third-party costs **[TO CONFIRM]** |
| 1–14 days before departure | 25% of payments made, less any non-recoverable third-party costs **[TO CONFIRM]** |
| Less than 24 hours before departure, or no-show | No refund |

**[TO CONFIRM: the owner should confirm or edit the 15–29 day and 1–14 day tiers above. The 30+ day tier is already stated on the live site.]**

## 4. Cancellation by you — day tours and single guide services

- Cancellation 48 hours or more before the start time: full refund, no fees.
- Cancellation within 48 hours of the start time, or no-show: no refund.
- This matches the "free cancellation up to 48 hours" promise shown on day-tour and guide pages.

## 5. Changes by you

- We will try to change your dates or route where suppliers allow. Supplier change fees are passed through at cost, and any price difference is confirmed with you before the change is applied.
- Where a date change is treated by a supplier as a cancellation and new booking, the tiers above may apply.

## 6. Cancellation by us

If we cancel a journey before departure (other than for events beyond our control):

- You receive a full refund of all payments made, or
- You may choose an alternative journey where available, transferring payments to it.

If a journey must stop early because of events beyond our control (section 7 of the Terms & Conditions), we will arrange comparable replacement services where possible and refund the value of services not delivered to the extent suppliers refund us.

## 7. Events beyond our control (force majeure)

When cancellation happens because of events beyond our reasonable control — war, civil unrest, government restrictions, border or flight closures, epidemics, natural disasters, or serious safety risks at the destination:

- We return to you everything recoverable from suppliers on your behalf.
- Where suppliers do not refund (for example, deposits hotels keep), we cannot refund those amounts, but we will offer a credit toward a future journey where possible. **[TO CONFIRM: credit validity period]**
- We recommend travel insurance that covers cancellations for medical, personal, and external reasons.

## 8. Travel insurance

Travel insurance is strongly recommended for every journey. **[TO CONFIRM: if insurance is mandatory, state it here.]** Insurance should cover cancellation, medical care, evacuation, and the activities in your itinerary. We are not liable for costs your insurance should have covered.

## 9. How refunds are paid

- Refunds are paid to the original payment account or method.
- Refund processing takes up to 14 business days after we confirm the refund, plus any time taken by banks or payment providers.
- Bank, transfer, or provider fees are deducted from the refund unless the cancellation was our fault.
- Refunds are paid in the currency of the original payment; exchange differences are not covered by us.

## 10. No-shows

If you do not arrive for the journey start without notice, the booking is treated as a no-show and payments are not refundable. If you expect to be late or delayed, contact us as soon as possible — we will help where we can, and your travel insurance may cover the loss.
